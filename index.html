The problem

Hearing damage depends on loudness and time together. NIOSH says every 3 dB increase halves the safe time: 85 dBA for 8 hours, 94 dBA for 1 hour, 100 dBA for 15 minutes. Mixed days are hard to judge by feel.

What it does

  • Build your day from activities with typical dBA levels, or enter your own readings
  • Dose as a percent of the NIOSH REL (85 dBA, 3 dB) and the OSHA PEL (90 dBA, 5 dB)
  • 8-hour equivalent level and the biggest contributor to your dose
  • "How long can I stay?" for any level, given what you already had today
  • Runs fully in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere
Educational estimate, not medical or compliance advice. Typical dBA values are approximate. Sources: NIOSH · OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95
