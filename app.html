How long can I stay?

AtdBA, I have -(NIOSH) /-(OSHA) left today.
NIOSH REL: 85 dBA for 8 hours, time halves for every 3 dB increase. OSHA PEL: 90 dBA for 8 hours, time halves for every 5 dB (29 CFR 1910.95, levels under 80 dBA ignored; 50% dose is the OSHA action level). Dose = sum of (time at level / allowed time at level). The listed dBA values are rough typical levels, not measurements: use a sound level meter or phone dosimeter app for your real sound. This is an educational estimate, not medical or compliance advice. Sources: NIOSH · OSHA 1910.95
